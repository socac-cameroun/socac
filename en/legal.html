2. Privacy Policy

SOCAC is committed to protecting the privacy of its visitors and customers. This policy explains what data we collect, why and how we use it.

Data Collected

When you use this site, we may collect the following data:

  • Via contact form: full name, email address, subject and message
  • Via WhatsApp: phone number and message content
  • Browsing data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, visit duration (via analytics cookies)

Purposes of Processing

  • Responding to your contact requests, quotes or orders
  • Improving our site and services
  • Informing you of our offers and news (with your consent)
  • Complying with our legal obligations

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract performance: processing your orders and requests
  • Consent: sending commercial communications
  • Legitimate interest: site improvement, security

Data Retention

Your personal data is kept for as long as strictly necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, and in any case for a maximum period of 3 years after your last contact with SOCAC.

Data Sharing

SOCAC never sells your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Formspree (contact form processing)
  • Google (web fonts and potentially analytics)
  • WhatsApp / Meta (direct communications you initiate)

These providers act as data processors and are subject to strict confidentiality obligations. Their respective privacy policies apply to their own data processing.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device (computer, tablet, mobile) when you visit our site.
